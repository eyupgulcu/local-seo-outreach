# One-page template (what the business receives)

Everything below uses invented placeholders. Replace with the audited business's real data only in the
user's private working files.

```
# Online visibility check: Example Electric Ltd., Sampletown
Date of check: 2026-01-10 | Prepared by: Jane Doe, Example Studio

## What we looked at
Your website, a web search for "Sampletown electrical wholesaler", your public maps listing, and what an AI
assistant with web search says when asked to recommend a wholesaler in Sampletown.

## What we saw
1. Website: opens on mobile, phone number is tappable. Title reads only "Example Electric". No product or
   brand pages.
2. Search: in our own web search, the first results were directories and news items. Your site was not among
   them. Google's own ranking may differ.
3. Maps: 4.6 stars from 31 reviews (as of the capture date; may have changed).
4. AI assistant: asked for wholesaler recommendations in Sampletown, it named two other shops. Asking again
   can return different names.

## The biggest gap
A customer who searches for a specific cable or breaker cannot see on your site that you stock it. Your
reviews show customers like you, but the site gives them no way to confirm availability before they call.

## What would fix it
A simple product and brand catalog with a title and description that include the city and the trade. We can
build this for you; the first step is a short call or a message to agree which product groups matter most.

## Limits of this check
Search results come from our own tool. Review counts are from the capture date. AI answers change from run to
run. We looked at public pages only.
```

## Rules for the one-pager

- One page. Plain language. No jargon the owner has to look up.
- Three or four observations, one gap, one offer.
- Each observation must be reproducible today by someone else.
- Do not write "you do not appear on Google" unless you saw that on Google itself.
- No numbers about future results: no traffic, ranking or revenue forecasts.
- Always include the limits paragraph.
